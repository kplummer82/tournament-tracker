Position
priorities

On each player's page, click a position tile once for primary (1°), again for secondary (2°), once more to clear.

  • Any mix works — a utility kid can carry four primaries.
  • Priorities feed the lineup picker's sort order, so the right names surface first at every position.
Why it matters: this one-time setup is what makes building a defense fast on game day.

Hard stop:
duplicates

One player can't hold two positions in the same inning. Both cells go red and Save locks until it's fixed.

  • This is the only rule that blocks saving — it's a data error, not a judgment call.

Optional fairness rules

Everyone plays. The app keeps score.

Two opt-in rules — off by default, toggled per game and team, saved the moment you click. They watch the innings in order, the way the game is actually played.

Fair sit

Everyone sits once before anyone sits twice.

No player takes a second bench inning while a teammate still hasn't had their first.

Fair outfield

Everyone plays outfield once before anyone plays it twice.

LF, CF, and RF innings spread across the whole roster before anyone repeats.

Off by defaultTurn them on only for the games where they apply.
RememberedSaved per game & team instantly — still set when you come back.
Drafts don't false-alarmOnly complete innings (all nine seats filled) are judged.

Warnings,
not roadblocks

Break a rule and the app names names — but Save stays live. It's the coach's call.

  • The amber banner says who repeats, and who's still waiting.
  • Outfield repeats tint their grid cell; sit repeats glow in the bench list.
  • Uncheck the rule and the warnings clear.

Under the hood

Small surface, room to grow

Scoped per game & team

Lineups, confirmations, and rule toggles are stored per game and per team — each dugout manages its own.

Saved on the spot

Rule toggles persist the moment they're clicked. Lineups save explicitly, so drafts stay drafts.

Open rulebook

Rules are stored as fair_sit / fair_outfield flags in an extensible set — new rules slot in without rework.

What's next League & division defaults. The same rules, set once at the league or division level, trickling down to every game — with the per-game toggles you've seen here as the override.
